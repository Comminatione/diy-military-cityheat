2031
Once the most fortified nation on Earth. Three weeks ago, Nova City went dark. No broadcasts. No signals. No one coming out.
Alpha-7 went in to find out why. They found NEXUS, military traitors, standing beside the Vreth. An alien force. A secret deal: Earth's cities, traded for power. No vote. No war. No warning. Alpha-7 lasted one hour.
One soldier walked out.

No moon tonight. NEXUS calls it bad luck. Reyes calls it cover. First confirmed Vreth ground presence in this sector — but ground was never the whole story.
Command's last transmission, before Voss went dark for good: "Echo-Prime is running something bigger than ground troops. Get eyes on it." Reyes got eyes on it. A shape blotted out the stars — low, silent, running without lights of its own.
The Vreth didn't need the moon.

2031 — DAY 9
Past the last supply road, the map turns white. NEXUS built a line across it anyway — bunkers in the permafrost, armor under snow tarps, a gunship on standby. They trusted the winter to do the guarding. Reyes walked in on foot.
Still nothing on Voss's channel. But NEXUS's own was wide open — cold makes people sloppy with their radios. "Convoy rolls at first light. Iron Vulture has the pass. Nobody climbs that ridge in this weather, so nobody's watching it." Somebody was about to be wrong.
The line was built to hold all winter.

Reyes only needs it to hold still.
"They brought armor to a blizzard.

2031 — DAY 11
The Vreth ship appears on no NEXUS manifest. High orbit, running dark, close enough to watch Nova City from above. Reyes hitched a ride on a supply shuttle.
Dock four. One unscheduled arrival, flagged by no one. The ship's AI logged the shuttle. It never logged what stepped off it. Echo-Prime was running something from in here. Reyes meant to find out what.
Off the map. Off the grid. Off the ledger.

2031 — DAY 14
The Vreth ship was only the wrapper. The real prize hangs above the equator: the comms relay every alliance channel runs through. They took it overnight. Reyes went up in a cargo pod.
No guards. No patrols. The station defends itself: turrets, drones, sealed doors, running a script nobody at NEXUS wrote. Every camera on the ring feeds one watcher. It's been counting. So has Reyes.
Cut the relay and the alliance goes quiet.

NEXUS ran off-world, sure that distance would save them. The Vreth survey team found this rock first. NEXUS built a command bunker beside their dig: a backup plan nobody told Earth about. Reyes landed in a dust storm.
Visibility near zero. NEXUS thinks that helps them. Bunker, runway, survey site, walking machines. Everything NEXUS has left, in one place. One soldier started this war. One soldier is about to end it.

◇ MUTATORS — HOW THEY WORK
Mutators are rule modifiers you pick before an Endless run. They make the game harder (or weirder) in exchange for a higher score multiplier. Select Endless Mode from the level grid, then toggle mutators in the Mutators card before deploying.
IRON MANNo health drops at all
SUDDEN DEATHOne hit kills you
TURBO2× enemy speed + spawn rate
GLASS CANNON+50% damage dealt, −50% HP
SCORE MULTMore active mutators = higher multiplier
